2. Service and requirements

IntoSquare provides a Figma plugin and a Chrome extension for importing selected designs as editable Squarespace blocks and transferring supported Site Styles. You need your own Figma and Squarespace access and permission to edit the destination website. Those services have separate terms and may charge their own fees.

Supported content is mapped to Squarespace’s available blocks and grid. Typography, spacing and some design features may differ from Figma. Review the import preview and keep backups before changing a live site. IntoSquare does not promise pixel-identical output for every design.

4. Plans and subscription billing

Pro includes unlimited supported block imports and Site Styles across your Squarespace websites, subject to these terms and reasonable security and service safeguards. Applicable taxes and the final amount are shown at checkout before you pay. Six-month subscriptions are charged for the full six months, rather than in monthly instalments.

Subscriptions renew automatically at the selected interval until cancelled. Changes to future renewal prices will be communicated before taking effect. Promotional or complimentary access may have different durations and does not itself create a paid subscription.
